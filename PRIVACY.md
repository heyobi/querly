# Querly — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Querly does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **No account, no sign-in, no network.** The app makes no network requests. The database engine (DuckDB) and its
  extensions run on your device.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Imported files, notebooks and databases are stored in the app's own storage on
  your iPhone (files and notebooks are visible to you in the Files app). They are deleted when you delete the app.
- **Sharing is your choice.** Data only leaves the device when you explicitly share or export a file
  (for example a result, a notebook or a database backup) through the iOS share sheet.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.**
- **Purchases** are handled by Apple through the App Store; the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.
- **TestFlight:** if you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to
  send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Contact: open an issue at <https://github.com/heyobi/querly/issues>.
